## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creater's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

• Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
• Provides logistics support.
• Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

• Better prices for farmers.
• Lower prices for consumers.
• Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
Explanation: A direct-to-buyer digital marketplace connecting farmers/FPOs with consumers and bulk buyers, backed by logistics and AI tools. 
Bullet How It Addresses the Problem: Removes middlemen to boost farmer income and lower consumer prices, uses AI for demand forecasting, and optimizes delivery routes. 
Bullet Innovation: Voice-first regional language support for farmers, pre-harvest buyer booking, and shared farm-gate pickups for small lots

## Technical Approach
Technologies: Flutter/React Native (farmer app), React/Next.js (buyer/FPO web), FastAPI/Node.js, PostgreSQL, Python ML (XGBoost/Prophet), routing APIs/OR-Tools, and UPI/SMS alerts. 
Bullet Methodology: Build voice/app crop listing and direct orders -> train AI demand/price forecasting -> add pickup clustering and route optimization -> integrate direct payouts and test prototype

## Feasibility and Viability
Feasibility: Practical using standard web/mobile/ML stacks, existing FPO hubs for batch collection, and low per-order service/logistics fees. 
Bullet Challenges: Low digital literacy, small farm quantities, weak rural network, and payment trust
Bullet Mitigation: Regional voice UI with FPO help, pooling nearby farm pickups, offline draft + SMS alerts, and fast UPI settlement.

## Impact and Benefits
Target Audience: Farmers/FPOs get better margins and demand insights; consumers/bulk buyers get fresher produce at lower prices. 
Bullet Benefits: Economic (higher farmer pay, lower buyer cost); Social (price transparency, less middleman reliance); Environmental (shorter routes cut fuel/emissions and less food spoilage).
